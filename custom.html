FAQ

Custom mode, answered.

What can I ask for?

Anything you’d type into a chatbot about some text: make flashcards, list key terms, turn plans into a to-do list. Wisp runs your instruction on whatever you drag over.

Can I change the shortcut?

Yes. Every shortcut in Wisp can be changed, including ⌃6.

Is Custom in Wisp Free?

Custom comes with Wisp Lifetime ($15 once) or Wisp Monthly ($2.99 a month with a 7-day free trial). Wisp Free includes Answer mode with 15 answers a day, so you can try how Wisp works first. It’s the same app: a license key unlocks the rest.

Are the answers always right?

AI can make mistakes, so double-check anything important. Wisp works best as a study buddy: use Explain to learn the why, not just the what.

Do I have to pay for the AI too?

No. Google Gemini has a free tier: paste a free key from Google AI Studio and you’re set. Since Wisp 1.4.4, a Setup Guide walks you through it the first time you open the app (or follow the step-by-step guide). You can also use your own OpenAI key, or run a model offline with Ollama or LM Studio.

FAQ

Rewrite mode, answered.

Does it change my language?

No. Rewrite keeps the same language. To change languages, use Translate (⌃3).

Where does the rewrite show up?

Right next to what you selected, like every Wisp answer, so you can compare it with your version and keep what you like.

Is it OK for schoolwork?

Use it on your own writing and follow your class’s rules on AI tools. Comparing your draft with the rewrite is a good way to learn what makes writing clearer.

Is Rewrite in Wisp Free?

Rewrite comes with Wisp Lifetime ($15 once) or Wisp Monthly ($2.99 a month with a 7-day free trial). Wisp Free includes Answer mode with 15 answers a day, so you can try how Wisp works first. It’s the same app: a license key unlocks the rest.

Do I have to pay for the AI too?

No. Google Gemini has a free tier: paste a free key from Google AI Studio and you’re set. Since Wisp 1.4.4, a Setup Guide walks you through it the first time you open the app (or follow the step-by-step guide). You can also use your own OpenAI key, or run a model offline with Ollama or LM Studio.

FAQ

Questions? Answered.

Do I have to pay for the AI too?

No. Google Gemini has a free tier that works great with Wisp: you paste a free key from Google AI Studio and you’re done (step-by-step guide). You can also use your own OpenAI key (you pay OpenAI per use) or run a model completely offline with Ollama or LM Studio.

Why does macOS ask me to click “Open Anyway” the first time?

Wisp isn’t in Apple’s paid developer program yet, so macOS asks you to confirm once: System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway. It takes about 30 seconds and the steps come with your download. After that it opens like any other app.

What does Wisp see on my screen?

Only the rectangle you drag, and only when you press the shortcut. It sends that picture plus the text it recognized on your Mac to the AI you picked, or to nobody if you use a local model. You can switch image sending off so only the text is sent.

Lifetime or monthly, which should I get?

Lifetime is $15 once and yours forever, updates included. Monthly is $2.99 with a 7-day free trial, which is nice if you want to try it first. Both unlock the exact same app.

How do I set it up after buying?

Gumroad emails you the download and a license key. Drag Wisp into Applications, open it, paste your license key and your AI key, and you’re set.

How do updates work?

Wisp updates itself. When a new version is out it offers to install it in one click, and you can check anytime with “Check for Updates…” in the menu.

Which Macs does it work on?

Any Mac running macOS 14 Sonoma or later, with Apple Silicon or Intel.

Can I change the shortcuts?

Yes, every one. Capture is ⇧⌘Space by default and each mode has its own shortcut, ⌃1 to ⌃6.

Are the answers always right?

AI can make mistakes, so double-check anything important. Wisp works best as a study buddy: use Explain to learn the why, not just the what.

I need help. Who do I talk to?

Reply to your Gumroad receipt email and it comes straight to the developer.

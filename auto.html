FAQ

Auto mode, answered.

How does Auto decide what to do?

It looks at what you selected and does the most useful thing: a question gets answered, another language gets translated and a long read gets summarized. If you want something else, switch modes in the answer panel.

Can I pick a mode myself instead?

Yes. Every mode has its own shortcut: ⌃1 Answer, ⌃2 Summarize, ⌃3 Translate, ⌃4 Rewrite, ⌃5 Explain and ⌃6 Custom. You can change every one of them.

What’s in Wisp Free?

Wisp Free includes Answer mode with 15 answers a day, no card needed. Wisp Lifetime ($15 once) or Monthly ($2.99 with a 7-day free trial) unlock every mode and unlimited answers.

Does it work on text I can’t select?

Yes. Wisp reads the pixels you drag over and recognizes the text on your Mac with Apple’s Vision framework, so it works on images, paused videos, screenshots and PDFs that are just pictures.

Do I have to pay for the AI too?

No. Google Gemini has a free tier: paste a free key from Google AI Studio and you’re set. Since Wisp 1.4.4, a Setup Guide walks you through it the first time you open the app (or follow the step-by-step guide). You can also use your own OpenAI key, or run a model offline with Ollama or LM Studio.
